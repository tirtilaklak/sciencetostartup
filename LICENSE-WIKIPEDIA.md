copy, company cards and linked profile supplements are by Wikipedia contributors
under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
These excerpts retain their source license; they are not relicensed under the
site's CC BY 4.0 or the software's MIT license. They fall within the quoted-source
exception already stated in `LICENSE-DATA.md`.

Each record and its rendered description retain the article, permanent revision,
author-history link, applicable license and an accurate extraction notice.
Sentence wording is unchanged; links, formatting and citation markers are omitted.
Preserve those credits and applicable share-alike terms when reusing the excerpts.

The current offering labels cite separate official sources. They are not presented
as Wikipedia quotations. Original research downloads and archived publication
