It covers our own work: the written statements, grades, labels, summaries and

## How to credit us

Name ScienceToStartup, give the dataset version, link to this license and say
whether you changed the data. For example:

> ScienceToStartup research data, version (the dataset version you used),
> https://sciencetostartup.com/method/. Licensed under CC BY 4.0. Changes: none.

Please also cite the original source of each figure you use. Each figure lists
its sources.
