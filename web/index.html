THE RESEARCH

How the research is checked.

Each claim links to its evidence. Financial figures retain their dates and the business they cover.

What the labels mean

Delivery evidence (public support for a company’s commercial activity) has several levels. Each rating covers the business described in that record.

Confidence grades

Each claim carries a research grade that says how strong its sources are.

A
Independent or buyer-side evidence, such as a filing or a confirmed customer.
B
Attributed company, investor, or named primary statement.
C
Secondary reporting, an estimate, or indirect evidence.
U
Unverified, undisclosed, or unresolved.

About, contact and corrections

ScienceToStartup is published by Musa Ozdem. It is a public-source research guide to the organizations that produce AI training data. No profiled company has paid for coverage, and none is a customer, investor or affiliate of the publisher.

Disclosure. The publisher is also proposing an AI research lab, described on the overview page. That proposal is separate from the research here, which applies the same rules to every organization.

Lab inquiries. Investors or AI labs interested in the proposed lab can email musaeminozdem@gmail.com.

Corrections. If a figure, date or listing is wrong, email musaeminozdem@gmail.com with the company name and the source. We review within seven days.

Personal data and removal. Company profiles name founders and executives from public company sources only. Profiles do not show personal contact details. If you are named here and want a record corrected or removed, email the same address. Visits are counted with GoatCounter, which sets no cookies.

Evidence labels

Evidence labels show who supports a claim. Buyer confirmations and company statements have separate labels. Confidence (how strongly the sources support a claim) has its own label.

Unverified claims remain labeled. Historical facts retain their dates. Research organizations are classified separately.
