What the labels mean

Confidence grades

Every claim gets a grade for how strongly its sources support it.

A
Independent or buyer-side evidence, such as a filing or a customer who confirms the work.
B
The company, an investor or another named first-hand source says it.
C
Reporting by others, an estimate, or indirect evidence.
U
Not confirmed. The fact may be undisclosed or still unresolved.

Tap or click a figure on a company page or in Compare to see its sources and grade.

Evidence labels. Each claim also names who stands behind it, such as Company-reported or Filed disclosure. A buyer’s confirmation and a company’s own statement get different labels.

Delivery evidence levels

Each company also gets a delivery evidence level (public proof that it sells and delivers its offer). The level covers one part of the business, such as the whole company or its AI data work.
