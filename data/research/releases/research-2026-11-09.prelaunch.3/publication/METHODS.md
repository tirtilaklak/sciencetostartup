Freeze membership before adjudication, preserve source-bound row decisions and independent receipts, and recompute metrics from rows. Test fixtures and generated accuracy summaries do not count.

Neither completed audit is supplied for this graph. The baseline therefore reports no achieved precision, recall, screening recovery or critical-attribution result. Datasheets for Datasets proposes reflective human documentation, asks about collection participants and compensation, and describes a development process refined over roughly two years. These are useful disclosure prompts, not evidence that every later datasheet follows them or that compensation is fair. The paper also notes documentation overhead and limits. This report uses those questions to expose unknowns while requiring source evidence for answers, rather than automatically converting a filled template into assurance.
