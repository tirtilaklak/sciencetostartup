<a id="finding-delivery-unit-before-provider"></a>
### Analytical judgment: delivery-unit-before-provider

In this reviewed sample, a useful supplier comparison begins with the delivery unit: participant access, licensed data, custom tasks or executable environments\.

Buyer implication: Specify who owns task design, annotation, QA, rights clearance and acceptance before comparing proposals\.

Limitations: First-party offering descriptions do not establish interchangeable services, capacity, realized quality or a market-wide supplier ranking\.

Confidence: Moderate for this sample’s disclosed distinctions; low for prevalence outside it\.

Reasoning: Prolific distinguishes participant access and managed collection; Snorkel describes refreshed non-exclusive products; Mercor describes custom task construction; Surge offers environments and verifiers\. Their responsibilities differ even where capability labels overlap\.

What would change this assessment: Matched statements of work and independently checked deliveries could show that particular offerings meet the same requirement and permit narrower outcome comparisons\.

### Analytical judgment: agreement-needs-denominators

Released agreement and preference-pair totals cannot be interpreted without the independent-judgment and exclusion denominators\.

Buyer implication: Request raw-to-retained counts, invalid-item rules, repeat labels, disagreement slices and corrected acceptance\.

Limitations: The reviewed workflows differ in task and sampling\. They cannot rank provider quality, and no common reliability measurement was executed\.

Confidence: High for the methodological distinction; low for transferring a numerical agreement result between workflows\.

Reasoning: HelpSteer3 selects agreeing labels and excludes disagreements; HelpSteer2 aggregates closest ratings; InstructGPT expands ranked sets into correlated pairs and usually uses one contractor per comparison\. Final record totals therefore summarize different observation processes\.

What would change this assessment: A common held-out task with independent raw judgments and the same exclusion rules could make reliability comparisons meaningful\.
