# Research methods

Evidence boundary and source hierarchy. The factual boundary is the approved, validated merged graph selected by the explicit partition index. Discovery candidates, provisional reviews, search snippets and unpinned working files do not become report facts. Primary creator papers, official repository/card versions, provider policies, audited statements and first-party case accounts retain their source type. Provider marketing is reported as a disclosure. A source-content review checks whether an exact claim is supported; it does not transform self-reported operations into an independent audit.

Assertion approval and review identity. Each approved assertion has exact-content author, source-verifier and adversarial receipts from separate actual AI invocations. Approval binds wording, subject, predicate, object or typed value, qualifications, source version, locator and excerpt digest. Report-level evidence-auditor and adversarial reviews are separate requirements bound to the exact report digest and complete graph digest. Prior report or graph reviews cannot approve this expanded synthesis. No fabricated human reviewer, honorific, institutional endorsement or review receipt is added.

Original-stage notes and current status. Frozen source records retain historical author-only or proposal-stage wording where it was part of the original packet. Those notes describe that earlier stage and do not replace current status. This report reads approval from exact-digest three-role receipts while retaining source-depth and substantive limitations. Twenty-six later-disputed bindings remain candidate/history records; approved replacements use new IDs after fresh independent review. The original scientific values and qualifications are unchanged, and earlier positive or negative receipts and actual capture/review dates are not erased.

Reading depth. full_source is a bounded editorial category: methods plus main results or worked examples and limitations, with separately recorded spans. It does not mean all appendices or proofs were read, every experiment reproduced or all corpus records checked. A truthful full_document_read false is compatible with adequate bounded main-section review. Abstract-only material cannot be promoted through a claim count. Dataset construction may be reviewed through creator methods and documentation without adding the original paper work as a fully reviewed paper or establishing corpus-byte identity.

Capture-specific evidence. Cogito and Mindy observations bind the exact saved official extracted-page snapshots available to the reviewers; origin HTML and live HTTP status were not exposed by that extraction. Separate direct retrieval attempts had their own failures and do not turn the extraction into a verified live-origin response. The Mindy FAQ is undated, so current staffing/location figures are not adopted. TrustScale’s rebrand article was decoded from the pinned public application bundle rather than established by rendered-page execution. These capture boundaries remain attached to the underlying source records and dossier limitations.

Version and identity accounting. A paper work counts once through its reviewed manifestation; a dataset or benchmark family counts once through approved family/release evidence. Formats, configs, splits and subset releases do not add families. FineWeb-Edu is currently one FineWeb subset release, correcting the initial eight-family baseline’s separate-family treatment without changing that archive’s bytes. MuSiQue’s new human-composed questions support one family; inherited seed corpora and answerability/serialization variants do not add families. Corporate aliases and product brands do not automatically add suppliers.

Computed coverage appendix. Coverage method 1.3.0 counts 1,713 approved assertions, 26 quarantined candidate assertions and 39 historical assertions. There are 1,790 scoped source records, of which 1,789 are captured; those captured records represent 503 distinct document versions under the version/response-hash rule. Multiple excerpts or locators can refer to one version and are not independent documents. The selected qualifying corpus contains 65 suppliers, 152 distinct paper works, 63 dataset families and 21 benchmark families. All 39 legacy organizations have completed bounded inclusion audits; 38 have conclusive commercial dispositions and BenchFlow remains commercially pending. These are separate measures, and no pending qualification contributes a qualifying supplier or frozen-core disposition.

Relationship appendix. Exactly 216 approved assertions in this input use explicit object relationships. Other observations use source-bound typed values. Every predicate is nontransitive: parent links support work/family accounting only. A paper mentioning a company, using its software or documenting expert recruitment does not authorize an unstated supplier edge, exclusive authorship, contract value or causal performance attribution. Paper versions, dataset releases, formats, aliases and mirrors remain identity evidence rather than automatically creating additional counted objects.

Launch gate appendix. The fixed floors require 39 completed legacy inclusion audits, 60 qualifying suppliers, 150 reviewed paper works, 60 dataset families, 20 benchmark families and disposition of at least 90% of a frozen eligible core. Commercial dispositions are separately reported; BenchFlow’s pending status does not invalidate its completed inclusion audit. The eligible core remains unfrozen. The formal reference and screening audits each require completed non-fixture holdouts and their source-bound, independently adjudicated provenance. Reference precision must reach 98% and recall 95% with no critical false attribution; screening needs at least 190 of 200 eligible records and at least 90% recall in each major stratum. Meeting numerical thresholds in a separate diagnostic does not complete these audits.

Audit status and limitations. Formal reference and screening launch audits are incomplete; no approval count, schema pass or diagnostic threshold comparison changes that state. The eligible inventory remains a lower bound under active independent boundary-policy review. Open discovery partitions, unresolved identities and mandatory deep-review gaps prevent a final denominator, census claim or core disposition rate. Accessible and well-documented sources can remain overrepresented despite a large approved corpus. A pinned authoring graph and a frozen diagnostic sample are distinct from a completed, frozen eligible inventory.

Search and selection bias. This is a selected public-source corpus rather than a probability sample. English-language material, public research artifacts and providers with detailed documentation are easier to discover and inspect. Confidential contracts, unavailable or blocked documents, undocumented suppliers and unobserved language/domain intersections can be missing. Unknown fields are preserved as unknown and do not count as evidence of absence. The report’s comparisons are sample-limited and should not be extrapolated into the entire market.

Source dates and weekly cutoff. published_on records a document’s publication date when established; the event described may be older; captured_on records retrieval; reviewed_on records actual review; assertion as_of records its declared observation date; an edition needs an actual publication confirmation. The planned 13 November 17:00 Pacific cutoff and 16 November weekly check remain future schedule points in this 10 November prelaunch preparation. A later capture cannot be moved into an earlier window, and a delayed run is catch-up rather than a backdated publication.

Interpretation and reproducible calculations. Approved counts are computed from qualifying identities and current approved assertions. Sample ratios should identify numerator and denominator before rounding. Project cost and acceptance formulas in chapter 8 are proposed methods with no invented inputs. Paper-reported results in the atlas retain the exact task, version and measurement condition described by the approved statement; they are not independently reproduced results, current model rankings or estimates of supplier performance. No metric is averaged across incompatible benchmark protocols.

The research hub is https://sciencetostartup.com/research/; the living report route is https://sciencetostartup.com/research/state-of-ai-data-labs/; the evidence and download index is https://sciencetostartup.com/research/data/; source-backed review and correction entry is https://sciencetostartup.com/review/. Download links in the prepared publication must bind its own JSON/CSV/SQLite, schemas, bibliography, checksum manifest, offline evidence appendix, PDF and reproduction package. This source report does not assert that the expanded matching package, DOI, Zenodo archive or Hugging Face mirror already exists.

Publication and corrections. Website preparation, deployment, archive confirmation and mirror synchronization have separate states and receipts. Accounts remain prepare-only under the current policy. A source-backed correction should identify the claim or report block, the source version and locator, the proposed wording and the reason. The research workflow reviews support, privacy and identity before integrating changes. Historical frozen packages retain pinned bytes; a correction belongs in a new version and change log rather than an invisible replacement.

Bibliography reading guide. The entries below provide the reviewed paper-work title, a source URL from its bound evidence and the inspected source version. Claim links carry the exact locator, excerpt hash, qualifications and review identities. A source URL can resolve to a later page; the recorded version and response digest define the reviewed content. Dataset and supplier bibliography records are available through each atlas/dossier’s claim bindings and the evidence ledger; no external affiliation is used as a substitute for a documented contribution.

Reference diagnostic, not launch audit. A private constructed challenge retains all 250 frozen cases sampled from the previously approved corpus. Two independent AI truth workers using the same requested model family established judgments with a third tie-breaker before comparison with the system under test; locator-context boundaries were clarified during adjudication. Under the strict consensus key, the unchanged run records 184 true positives, three false positives, two false negatives and 61 true negatives: precision 184/187 = 98.3957% and recall 184/186 = 98.9247%. These are challenge-specific results, not production accuracy, discovery recall, human peer review or completed reference-audit evidence.

Locator reproduction. A locator identifies pinned source bytes and an extraction convention. One-based physical PDF pages differ from printed labels; global marked-text lines count markers and blank lines, while per-page pdftotext-layout coordinates use their declared physical page and tool convention. Preserve source versions, raw-response hashes and supplied derived-text hashes before interpreting a line range. Dataset-card revisions document the inspected card and do not automatically identify every corpus shard. A successful re-extraction is reproducibility evidence, rather than a new capture, scientific finding or independent content review.
