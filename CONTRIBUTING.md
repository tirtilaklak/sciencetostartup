# Contributing research evidence

ScienceToStartup publishes independent public-source research. A submission is a
candidate for review, not an automatically accepted fact or endorsement.

Use the issue chooser to correct a claim, review a section, or show public reuse.
Provide an edition/page URL, stable claim or section identifier, precise source
locator, proposed correction and the scope of the evidence. Explain relevant
affiliations and conflicts. Do not upload private records or contact information.

We review corrections within seven days. Duplicate reports are joined to the
existing issue. A material unresolved objection can hold a finding or edition.
The disposition records the evidence, reasoning, actual reviewer type and date.
Closed issues remain part of the correction history.

Public credit requires explicit consent and identifies the specific contribution,
edition and section. A self-reported affiliation is not verified expertise or an
organization's endorsement. Automated research is labeled Separate AI review;
no human review is claimed unless a person actually performed it.

The original research and text are CC BY 4.0, subject to LICENSE-DATA.md's
third-party and personal-information exclusions. Code is MIT. Cite the exact
edition and original evidence, and describe transformations when reusing data.

The existing corrections contact is available on the method page for people who
cannot use GitHub. Invitations and reviewer outreach are prepared as drafts;
this contribution workflow does not authorize sending unsolicited messages.
