## Review brief

Select a specific edition and section. Check material claims against their pinned
sources, challenge important omissions and alternatives, inspect calculations,
and explain where a buyer could misread the conclusion. Distinguish disclosure
from proven performance, historical from current evidence, and capabilities from
documented supplier contributions. Submit exact claim IDs and source locators.

State relevant interests and whether credit is permitted. Acknowledgement names
the actual review scope and is not an affiliation or employer endorsement.

focuses on factual support, important omissions, methodology and practical buyer
implications. The full evidence and limitations are public. You can submit comments
through https://sciencetostartup.com/review/ and choose whether to receive scoped
public credit. Participation is voluntary and does not imply your employer's
endorsement. No response will be represented as approval.

Replace the bracketed scope with a real published section before seeking approval
to send. Sending any invitation requires separate explicit messaging authority.
