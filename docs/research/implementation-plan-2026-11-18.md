## Prioritized user journeys

**P0 — trust the released object.** A visitor receives one tested artifact, including its evidence and working downloads. Reject ambiguous upload outcomes and conflicting artifact identities before promotion. The release is held when a mandatory check cannot run; an earlier component pass cannot certify a later merged artifact.

**P1 — decide whether a work route fits.** Start at `/work/`, search an exact organization or role, read pay units and geography, then open the source. Exercise Mercor's standing-network context, AfterQuery's pay conflict and Appen's source conditions at 320px. A network is not a guaranteed project; annual salary is not hourly pay; a testimonial's country is not eligibility. Corrections receive field-scoped receipts without silently refreshing unrelated conditions.

**P1 — inspect a research claim.** Start at `/research/papers/`, find the work, open a specific version, follow an assertion to its source record and review receipt. A reader must distinguish approved from candidate content before acting. Full titles, source-version identity and limitations survive narrow screens, search and sharing metadata.

**P2 — return to useful reading.** Start at `/daily/`, open a dated edition, inspect a selection's findings and limits, and copy its permanent URL. Subscribe through generated `/daily/feed.xml` or `/daily/feed.json`. These paths are implemented in [daily_scan.py](../../scripts/daily_scan.py); visible follow links and head alternates advertise them. No email subscription is offered without an actual delivery and unsubscribe system.

## Thirty days after verified release

Day 1 begins after activation is verified. Musa owns editorial decisions; release and source-review responsibilities remain separate. Budget 2.5–3 hours weekly for distribution and measurement after the release QA pass. Primary-source research and correction review are additional; hold unsupported material rather than fill a quota.

| Window | Work, effort and reviewable output |
|---|---|
| **Days 1–3** | Spend 60–90 minutes checking dated destinations, feed parsing, public media types, follow controls and correction entry points. Save the released route inventory and observation time. Resolve release blockers before acquisition. |
| **Days 4–7** | Spend 60–90 minutes preparing one source-backed LinkedIn finding: one original evidence table, a bounded conclusion and a dated owned-site link. Prepare the profile description correction documented in the [legacy review](legacy-platform-migration-review-2026-11-18.md). No profile edit or post is performed by this plan. |
| **Week 2** | Prepare a second finding and review the existing GitHub README, citation, feed and correction paths; about 90 minutes. Add one reproducible reuse example to the review queue. Prepared release assets stay labelled prepared until matching public receipts exist. |
| **Week 3** | Spend about two hours reviewing the Hugging Face card/files/license/revision package and a third evidence note. The card must explain record grain, candidates, historical scope and reuse limits. Existing metadata preparation does not establish mirror publication. |
| **Week 4 and Day 30** | Prepare one further useful note; review a complete measurement window and next month's topic priorities, about two hours. Retain corrections and held outputs. Choose the next topic using demonstrated reading/reuse and editorial value, without a traffic forecast. |

Rank channels **owned site and feeds first**, LinkedIn second for concise professional findings, GitHub for reproducibility, then Hugging Face for dataset reuse. Keep X paused unless the publisher explicitly changes that preference. No accounts, messages, posts or schedules are created here. Retired commercialization routes need exact reviewed mappings or honest retirement explanations; homepage redirects do not establish equivalence.

## Measurement without another beacon

Keep the existing Cloudflare Analytics integration and GitHub repository. GA is not established; add no GA beacon. Cloudflare's [documented limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/faq/) exclude custom-event and UTM tracking; adaptive samples and dimension sums require care. Do not publish private analytics.

| KPI | Definition and decision |
|---|---|
| **Journey acceptance** | Passed required scenarios / executed required scenarios, with blocked scenarios listed separately. Release requires every mandatory scenario to pass on the final artifact. |
| **Review-date coverage** | Sourced field slots with an actual scoped review date / all sourced field slots in the stated snapshot. Track unknown dates explicitly; build time never increases coverage. |
| **Delivery integrity** | Validated actual editions / attempted editions, with held and delayed attempts recorded. Separately monitor real feed response/parse failures. Polls are not subscribers. |
| **Useful reading** | Compare equal dated Cloudflare windows for known released content paths and available referrers. Classify unmatched/legacy paths separately; today's 404 does not prove its historical status. Views are not unique applicants or conversions. |
| **Discovery and reuse** | Record owner-verified indexing observations and dated, attributable external reuse examples separately. Sitemap acceptance is not indexing; downloads, stars and prepared internal examples are not adoption. Unknown owner coverage stays unknown. |
