# Inclusion audits and commercial dispositions

Coverage method 1.3 corrects an implementation mismatch in the agreed
operating contract. The launch minimum is an inclusion audit of every one of the
39 existing organizations. A source review may finish with commercial supplier
qualification unresolved. Earlier progress reporting labeled only conclusive
inclusion/exclusion dispositions as completed audits.

`legacy_inclusion_audits` requires full-source depth and subject-owned approved
source-content evidence. Normal graph validation still requires actual passing
author, source-verifier and adversarial receipts for each approved assertion.
Metadata, historical descriptions, candidate assertions and missing reviews do
not count. The launch minimum remains 39; generated policy cannot lower it.

`legacy_dispositions` remains a separate count of conclusive included, excluded
or historical-only dispositions. A pending commercial qualification contributes
no qualifying supplier and no disposed frozen-core record. The supplier minimum
of 60, paper minimum of 150, dataset minimum of 60, benchmark minimum of 20,
90 percent frozen-core requirement, source cutoff, both actual 200-record audits,
synthesis reviews, archive and exact-artifact checks are unchanged.

The method version and policy identity change explicitly. Earlier archived
methods and bytes remain frozen, including their original metric names and
calculations. No entity status, assertion, review receipt, canonical input or
historical archive is changed by this correction. This correction passed a separate AI contract/gate review and targeted technical
checks before integration. It creates no supplier qualification, new source
review, completed accuracy audit or public release.
